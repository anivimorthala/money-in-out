Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Money In & Out is a personal finance application. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Local data

Financial records such as transactions, categories, payment methods and balances are intended to be stored locally on your device unless you explicitly use an export, import or backup feature.

Google Sign-In and Google Drive

If you choose Google Sign-In or Google Drive backup, the app may request the Google permissions shown on Google's consent screen. Google account information is used to identify the selected account and provide the requested backup or synchronization functionality.

Backup data is used to provide the backup and restore functionality you request. Money In & Out does not sell your Google account information or financial records.

Exports and imports

The app may let you export or import supported formats such as CSV or PDF. Files are handled according to where you choose to save or share them.

Data sharing

Money In & Out does not sell personal information. Information is not shared with third parties except where necessary to provide a feature you explicitly use, such as Google services, or where required by law.

Security

The app includes security features such as app lock and protected local data handling. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.
